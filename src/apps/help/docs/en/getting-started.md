# Getting started

Nexus Arcade OS is a desktop in your browser. Everything you make stays in this browser, and it
works offline once loaded.

## The desktop

- **Top bar:** the focused app, your workspaces (the dots), status icons, notifications and the
  clock. Click your avatar for the profile menu (lock, switch profile, log out).
- **Dock:** pinned and open apps. Right-click an app for its windows, pin or unpin it.
- **Desktop:** files from your Desktop folder. Right-click for a new folder, a text file, the
  wallpaper or widgets.

## Opening apps

| How                                            | What                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> (launcher)    | Browse all apps                      |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (command palette) | Find apps, commands, files, settings |
| Double-click a file                            | Opens it with its app                |
| [App Center](help:nexus.appcenter)             | Install and uninstall apps           |

## Windows

Drag a window by its title bar. Drop it on a screen edge or corner to snap it, or hover the
maximize button for layouts. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd>/<kbd>→</kbd>/<kbd>↑</kbd>/<kbd>↓</kbd>
snap the focused window with the keyboard. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>`</kbd> switches windows,
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> shows all of them.

## Your files

The [File Explorer](help:nexus.files) shows your home folder with Documents, Pictures, Music and
more. Drop files from your computer into it to bring them into Nexus. The
[Terminal](help:nexus.terminal) works on the same files.

## Make it yours

[Settings](help:nexus.settings) (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>) has themes, accent colors,
wallpapers, text size, sounds and much more. In any app, <kbd>F1</kbd> opens its help page.
