# Questions and answers

## Where are my files?

In this browser's storage (IndexedDB), in your profile. Open them in the
[File Explorer](help:nexus.files). They don't appear on your computer's disk; use **Download**
in the File Explorer, or a backup, to get them out.

## I opened Nexus in another browser and everything is gone.

Each browser (and Nexus Lite) has its own storage. Make a backup in one and restore it in the
other.

## Do reminders ring when Nexus is closed?

No. Reminders and alarms only ring while Nexus is open in a browser tab. Nexus tells you about
the ones you missed when you come back.

## An app stopped working.

Its window shows "This app stopped working" with **Restart** and **Copy report**. The rest of
Nexus keeps running. The System Monitor can also end a window (**End task**).

## How much space can Nexus use?

That depends on the browser and your disk; Settings → Storage shows the current numbers.

## Some keyboard shortcuts don't work.

Browsers keep some keys for themselves. See [Keyboard shortcuts](help:general/shortcuts); you can
also choose your own keys in Settings → Shortcuts.

## Is Nexus open source?

Yes, under the MIT license. Settings → About links to the source code.
