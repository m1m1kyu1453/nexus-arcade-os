# App Center

All apps that come with Nexus, sorted by category. Search by name or keyword at the top left.

Choose an app to see its **screenshots**, what's new in it (**changelog**), its size, how much data
it stores, which file types it opens, its commands and the **permissions** it uses.

- **Uninstall** hides an app everywhere: the launcher, the dock, search and the command palette.
  Tick "Also delete its data" to remove its documents, history and settings as well; files in
  your folders are never touched. Uninstalled apps are listed under **Uninstalled**, and
  **Install** brings them back.
- **Clear data** deletes an app's stored data but keeps the app.
- **Settings** opens the app's settings in Settings → Apps.

The App Center itself can't be uninstalled (nothing could bring it back).
