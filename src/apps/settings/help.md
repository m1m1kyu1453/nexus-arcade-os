# Settings

Every option of Nexus in one place. Open it from the launcher, with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>,
or from the command palette: type `@` followed by a setting's name to change it right there or
jump to it here.

## Finding a setting

Type in the search box at the top left (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>). Results show single
settings and pages; pick one to jump there; the setting is highlighted. Changes are saved
immediately. The ↺ button next to a setting resets it to its default.

## Pages

- **Personalization:** theme (or Auto to follow your system), accent color, wallpaper (gallery,
  your own picture, slideshow from a folder), text size, transparency, animations, battery saver
  and sounds.
- **Desktop & Dock:** dock position, size, auto-hide and magnification; widgets and workspaces.
- **Windows:** snapping to screen edges and the snap layouts on the maximize button.
- **Notifications:** Do not disturb, system notifications outside Nexus, which apps may notify you.
- **Shortcuts:** every keyboard shortcut. Choose **Change** and press the new keys; shortcuts
  used twice are marked.
- **Language & Region:** the language of Nexus and, separately, how dates and numbers look.
- **Profiles & Lock:** your name and color, a PIN or password, automatic locking, other profiles.
  The lock keeps others out of your profile on a shared computer, but it does not encrypt data.
- **Storage:** how much space Nexus uses, per app and per folder; clear an app's data; ask the
  browser to keep data permanently; records that could not be read are listed (never deleted
  without asking).
- **Apps:** each app's own settings, and the apps chosen with "Always use this app".
- **Privacy:** what is kept about your activity (clipboard, recent files, notifications, search,
  log) and buttons to clear it. Nothing ever leaves your browser unless you set it up.
- **Accessibility:** high contrast, text size, a stronger focus ring, larger buttons, reduced
  motion, sounds and announcements for screen readers.
- **Backup & Restore:** download everything as one `.nexus-backup` file, optionally encrypted
  with a password. Restoring shows the file's contents first; choose to replace this profile's
  data or merge. A reminder can ask you to back up regularly.
- **Updates:** the installed version, checking for a new one and what changed.
- **About:** version, edition, links, Nexus Lite download and the licenses of everything
  Nexus is built with.
